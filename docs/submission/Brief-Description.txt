Project Irene began with a personal experience: needing therapy in college, finding it expensive, and not wanting to worry our parents. We wanted the first step towards support to feel more affordable and less intimidating.

Irene is a working prototype for private intake and therapist-led group support. Participants describe their needs privately, review an AI-assisted summary, and choose whether to join a professionally approved group with an upfront shared session fee. Matching considers practical constraints and related concerns and goals; a therapist makes the suitability decision. Participants retain control through summary correction, invitation choices, and intake export or deletion.

Firebase Hosting serves the React interface. Cloud Run hosts the Java/Spring Boot workflow. Gemini drafts intake summaries and private facilitator preparation, and its embeddings support similarity matching. Secret Manager protects runtime credentials. Firestore is not currently used: Supabase PostgreSQL stores application data. Google Stitch supported the coral interface design.

For the Sustainability & Social Impact theme, our intended contribution is a lower financial barrier, more agency over disclosure, and access to professionally guided community. The prototype has been tested with fictional data; real-user impact and clinical suitability remain to be evaluated.
