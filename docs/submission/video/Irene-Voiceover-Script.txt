Support can be expensive. And sharing personal concerns with a room full of strangers can feel difficult. Meet Irene: a private first step toward affordable, therapist led group support.

The idea is simple. One therapist leads a suitable group, and the session fee is shared. For example, a twenty four hundred rupee session split between six people is four hundred rupees each. Actual fees and group sizes are set by the therapist.

Here is the live app, using a fictional participant. The person chooses a program and begins a private intake. Other participants cannot read their story.

They set their availability, sharing comfort, and maximum session budget. These practical constraints matter alongside shared concerns, so a promising match also needs to work in everyday life.

With explicit consent, the participant describes their concerns and goals. In this example, a fictional student wants more confidence speaking in class and a stronger sense of connection.

Gemini turns that intake into a draft summary and structured matching features. The participant can correct the summary before confirming it for their chosen therapist. Possible risk or uncertainty signals are routed for human review.

Next, a verified therapist reviews the confirmed intake. They decide whether the person should be considered for a group or needs an individual conversation. AI does not diagnose or replace that judgment.

Google embeddings help compare the meaning of concerns and goals. Irene combines similarity with practical constraints to suggest a circle. The therapist reviews the proposal, sets the session details, and approves invitations.

The participant sees the session and exact individual quote, then accepts or declines. This demo collects no payment. The therapist can also generate a private AI preparation brief for the group.

Participants can review, export, or delete their intake data. Access is restricted by account role and assignment. The demonstration uses fictional information, and is not a clinical or emergency service.

Irene is live on Firebase Hosting and Google Cloud Run, with Gemini, Secret Manager, and Supabase PostgreSQL. Thirty backend tests and thirty five live workflow checks passed. Clinical validation remains a next step.

Irene makes the first step private, the grouping thoughtful, and the session cost shared. A little less alone. Explore the live prototype.
